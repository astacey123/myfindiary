Assumes a standard capital repayment mortgage at a fixed rate for the whole term. Most UK lenders cap penalty-free overpayments (often around 10% of the balance per year) and may charge an Early Repayment Charge during a fixed-rate deal — check your mortgage terms before overpaying.
