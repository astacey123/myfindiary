2. What Financial Diary is

Financial Diary is two things: a set of calculators, and a record.

The calculators take figures you type in — a mortgage balance and rate, a pension pot and contribution, an investment sum and an assumed growth rate, your savings — and do arithmetic on them, showing the result as a number and a chart. The record is your diary: whenever you press “Log this to my diary”, a dated snapshot of what you were looking at is saved to your account, so you can come back in six months and see what's changed. The Tracker and Goal Tracker pages do the same thing for check-ins you log over time.

That's the whole of it. There is no bank connection, no product comparison, no recommendation engine, and nothing that acts on your behalf. You type figures in; it does maths on them; it remembers what you typed.

4. Projections are estimates, not forecasts

This matters more than anything else on the page, so it's worth being blunt about it.

Every forward-looking figure the site shows you — a pension pot at 67, a net worth in twenty years, an investment total, the interest saved by overpaying — is arithmetic performed on assumptions that you chose. The growth rates, the interest rates, the contribution amounts, the term lengths: all of them are figures you typed into a box or dragged on a slider. Change one of them and the answer changes with it.

Consequently:

  • The projections are not a prediction of what your money will actually do, and they are not a promise, guarantee or forecast of any outcome.
  • They assume conditions that stay steady for decades. Real interest rates move, real investments fall as well as rise, real inflation erodes real money, real tax rules change, and real lives take turns nobody modelled.
  • The mortgage tools assume a capital repayment mortgage at a single fixed rate for the whole term. Most real mortgages don't work like that, and a lender's own illustration is the figure that counts.
  • They take no account of tax, charges, platform fees, adviser fees or product-specific rules unless you have built those into the figures you entered yourself.
  • Anything wrong that you type in comes out the other end looking just as confident as anything right. The site cannot tell the difference.

Please don't make an irreversible financial decision on the strength of a number this site produced without checking it against the actual paperwork and, where it matters, a professional.
